
# Session #27 Intimacy with God: Victory in the Face of Betrayal (Pt. 1)

>“The friend of the Bridegroom…rejoices greatly because of the Bridegroom’s voice.” (Jn. 3:29)

## I. REVIEW: THE END-TIME CHURCH IS TRANSFORMED IN CONTEXT TO BETRAYAL

A. The combination of an unprecedented outpouring of the Spirit with betrayal is part of the optimum environment for the Church to be transformed in order to relate as an unified international family of affection (Jn. 17:22-23). Multitudes in the end-time Church will be progressing toward mature love, while many will be overcome with offense that will lead them to betray other believers (Mt. 24:10).

><sup>22</sup>“And <ins>the glory</ins> which You gave Me I have given them, that <ins>they may be one</ins> just as We are one: <sup>23</sup>…that <ins>the world may know</ins> that You have sent Me…” (Jn. 17:22-23)

><sup>10</sup>“And then <ins>many</ins> will be <ins>offended</ins>, will <ins>betray</ins> one another...<sup>12</sup>the love of <ins>many</ins> will grow cold… <sup>14</sup>This gospel…will be preached in <ins>all the world</ins>…then the end will come.” (Mt. 24:10-14)

><sup>16</sup>“You will be <ins>betrayed</ins> even by <ins>parents</ins> and brothers, <ins>relatives</ins> and <ins>friends</ins>…” (Lk. 21:16)

B. God's power will be openly manifest in an unprecedented measure in the final 3½ years of this age (Rev. 12:6, 10, 14). At that time one of Satan’s most destructive weapons will be accusation.

><sup>6</sup>The woman fled into the wilderness…[for] <ins>one thousand two hundred and sixty days</ins> [3½ years]. <sup>7</sup>…Michael and his angels <ins>fought</ins> with the dragon… <sup>9</sup>The dragon…and his angels were cast out… <sup>10</sup><ins>Then</ins> I heard a loud voice…“<ins>Now</ins> salvation, strength…and <ins>the power of His Christ have come</ins>, for the <ins>accuser</ins> of our brethren, who <ins>accused them</ins>…day and night, has been cast down. <sup>11</sup>And they <ins>overcame him</ins> by the <ins>blood</ins> of the Lamb and by the word of their testimony, and they <ins>did not love their lives</ins> to the death. <sup>12</sup>…Woe to the inhabitants of the earth and the sea! For the devil has come…having great wrath, because he knows that <ins>he has a short time</ins>.” (Rev 12:6-12)

><sup>28</sup>“…I will pour out My Spirit <ins>on all flesh</ins>; your sons and your daughters <ins>shall prophesy</ins>… <sup>30</sup>I will show <ins>wonders</ins> in the heavens and in the earth: blood and fire and pillars of smoke. <sup>31</sup>The <ins>sun</ins> shall be turned into darkness, and the <ins>moon</ins> into blood, <ins>before</ins> the coming of the great and awesome day of the LORD... <sup>32</sup>and in Jerusalem <ins>there shall be deliverance</ins>… (Joel 2:28-32)

C. Jesus wanted them to know that some would be betrayed in being put out of their spiritual families (synagogues; 16:2). In context to betrayal, the Spirit will escort us into the beauty of Jesus (16:13).

><sup>26</sup>When the Helper [the Spirit] comes…He will <ins>testify of Me</ins> [reveal more of Me]. <sup>27</sup>And <ins>you will bear witness</ins>… <sup>16:2</sup>They will <ins>put you out of the synagogues</ins> [betrayal]… ” (Jn. 15:26-16:2)

><sup>13</sup>The Spirit of truth…will <ins>guide</ins> [escort] you into all truth…<sup>14</sup>He will <ins>glorify</ins> Me, for He will take of what is Mine and <ins>declare it to you</ins>. (Jn. 16:13-14)

D. Those relating in godly ways contend for the destiny, honor, and family blessings of others. Hundreds of millions will respond to betrayal in a godly way resulting in the supernatural end-time unity of the Church. What Satan means for evil, the Lord uses for good (Gen. 50:20)

><sup>44</sup>I say to you, <ins>love</ins> your enemies, <ins>bless</ins> those who curse you, <ins>do good</ins> to those who hate you… <sup>45</sup>that <ins>you may be sons of your Father</ins>…for He makes His sun rise on <ins>the evil</ins>… (Mt. 5:44-45)
