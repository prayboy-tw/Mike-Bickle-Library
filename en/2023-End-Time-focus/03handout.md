## II. 5 REASONS WE VALUE STUDYING WHAT THE BIBLE SAYS ABOUT THE END TIMES

A. ***Reason #1—unique dynamics:*** The generation of Jesus’ return will have unique dynamics — unprecedented _pressures_ (shaking, sin, darkness) and an unprecedented _outpouring of the Spirit_. This will create a situation in which the nations will be afraid and confused searching for answers.

><sup>2</sup>For behold, the <ins>darkness shall cover the earth</ins>, and deep darkness the people; but the Lord will arise over you, and <ins>His glory will be seen upon you</ins>. (Isa. 60:2)

B. ***Reason #2—biblical reasons:*** By far the _most described_ generation in Scripture is the generation that will be alive when Jesus returns. It is the main focus in over ***150 chapters in the Bible***  
(see _mikebickle.org/resources/resource/2888_ for a list of these chapters).

C. ***Reason #3—prophetic reasons:*** The biblical signs of the times include events and trends that are predicted in Scripture. Jesus, the apostles, and the OT prophets prophesied _signs of the times_ — these signs will alert God’s people (who are alive in the generation of Jesus’ return) of His soon return.

1. Jesus ***commanded*** one generation to ***“know”*** that His return was near—this command was for the generation living when ***“all”*** 25 signs prophesied in Matthew 24 and Luke 21 occur.

><sup>33</sup>When you <ins>see all</ins> these things [in 24:3-28], <ins>know</ins> that it [His return] is near—at the doors… <sup>34</sup><ins>This generation will by no means pass away</ins> till all these things [in 24:29-31] take place… <sup>36</sup>Of that day and hour no one knows… (Mt. 24:33-36)

2. ***The timing:*** In the generation that Jesus returns, ***“all”*** 25 signs will accelerate in a way that can be ***“seen”*** by the global Body of Christ. Now, for the first time in history, most of these 25 signs of the times are increasing and making headline news on a global level.

3. The Lord indicated that a generation may last up to 100 years (Gen. 15:13-16).

D. ***Reason #4—the promise of increased understanding (Jer. 23:20; Dan. 11:33).*** There is power in knowledge. Since the Lord can accurately foretell the details of His end-time plan, we know that He has power over them and is able to use them redemptively for the good of His people.

1. The Lord will raise up ***“people of understanding”*** who will help “many” who will be hungry to understand the biblical narrative related to the end times (Dan. 11:33; 12:10).

><sup>33</sup>“And those of the <ins>people who understand</ins> [end-time messengers] shall <ins>instruct many</ins>; yet for many days they shall <ins>fall</ins> [be martyred] by sword and flame, by captivity… <sup>35</sup>to <ins>refine</ins> them, <ins>purify</ins> them…until the <ins>time of the end</ins>.” (Dan. 11:33-35)

2. God will give His people ***“perfect,”*** or mature, understanding of His end-time judgments

><sup>20</sup>The anger of the Lord [His judgments] will not turn back until He has executed… the thoughts of His heart. In the <ins>latter days</ins> you will <ins>understand it perfectly</ins>. (Jer. 23:20)

E. ***Reason #5—we are alerted by 4 pastoral challenges:*** People must understand the ***biblical narrative*** for _what_ will happen and _why_ it will happen, or they will by default embrace a ***secular narrative*** of the events, making them more vulnerable to being overcome by fear, offense, lust, and deception.

F. Jesus highlighted ***4 responses*** that will greatly challenge many people’s faith in the end times:
***F.O.L.D.***—_**F**ear_ (Lk. 21:26), _**O**ffense_ (Mt. 24:10), _**L**ust_ (Lk. 21:34), and _**D**eception_ (Mt. 24:11)

><sup>26</sup>“…men’s hearts failing them from <ins>fear</ins>… <sup>34</sup>“But take heed to yourselves, lest your hearts be weighed down with <ins>carousing</ins> [lust, immorality, etc.], <ins>drunkenness</ins>… and that Day [Jesus’ second coming] come on you unexpectedly.” (Lk. 21:26, 34)

><sup>10</sup>“Then many will be <ins>offended</ins>... <sup>11</sup>Many false prophets will rise up and <ins>deceive</ins> many.” (Mt. 24:10-11)

1. ***Fear:*** The hearts of people will be overcome with fear (Lk. 21:26), if they are not properly equipped in the Word and walking in the Spirit. This applies to believers and unbelievers.

2. ***Offense:*** Many will be offended at God and one another. Some will be offended at Jesus’ leadership—since they were taught that they would be raptured before the trouble intensified.

3. ***Lust:*** The word _carousing_ speaks of self-indulgence, with the main idea being immorality. Often people who are overwhelmed by fear, anxiety, or offense seek relief from their stress in drunkenness, drugs, immorality, etc. They “self-medicate” with lust seeking relief from pain.

4. ***Deception:*** Some of the most dangerous false prophets will be popular _social commentators_ and _media personalities_ who are dignified, good-looking, caring, intelligent, and well-spoken. They will promote a social narrative that is contrary to the biblical narrative and values.

G. The escalating fear, offense, lust, and deception will result in many falling away from Jesus. Paul prophesied of a “falling away” in the end times (1 Tim. 4:1-2; 2 Tim. 3:1-7; 4:3-5; cf. Heb. 6:4-6; 10:26-31, 39; 2 Pet. 2:1-3, 20-22; 3:17). This will be one of the most tragic realities in history. Paul presented it as being as obvious and significant as the appearing of the Antichrist on the world stage.

><sup>3</sup>Let no one deceive you by any means; for <ins>that Day</ins> [Jesus’ return] will not come unless <ins>the falling away</ins> comes first and the <ins>man of sin</ins> [Antichrist] is revealed. (2 Thes. 2:3)
