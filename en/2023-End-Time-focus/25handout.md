
# Session #25 Seven Situations in Israel During the Tribulation (Pt. 1)

>“The friend of the Bridegroom…rejoices greatly because of the Bridegroom’s voice.” (Jn. 3:29)

## I. ISRAEL REFINED: 7 SITUATIONS FOR JEWS IN THE LAND IN THE 3½ YEARS

A. The Lord will bring 1/3 of Israel through the fire to refine them in bringing them to salvation. It is possible that over 5-6 million Jews worldwide will look to Jesus for salvation in the end times.

><sup>9</sup>I will bring the <ins>one-third through the fire</ins>, will <ins>refine</ins> them…and <ins>test</ins> them as gold is tested. They will call on My name…and each one will say, “The LORD is my God.” (Zech. 13:9)

><sup>26</sup>And so <ins>all Israel will be saved</ins>, as it is written… (Rom. 11:26)

B. The time of intense difficulty for Israel in the end times will not last longer than 42 months (Dan. 12:1, 7; Mt. 24:21-22; cf. Dan. 7:25; 9:27; 12:7; Rev. 11:2-3; 12:6, 14; 13:5).

><sup>1</sup>“At that time <ins>Michael</ins> shall stand up…there shall be a <ins>time of trouble</ins>, <ins>such as never was</ins> since there was a nation…at that time <ins>your people shall be delivered</ins>… <sup>7</sup>I heard the man [angel]… he <ins>swore</ins> by Him …it shall be for a <ins>time, times, and half a time</ins> [42 months]; and when the power of the holy people has been <ins>completely shattered</ins>, all these things shall be finished. (Dan. 12:1, 7)

><sup>15</sup>“…<ins>when</ins> you see the ‘<ins>abomination of desolation</ins>,’ spoken of by Daniel…<sup>16</sup>then let those who are in Judea <ins>flee to the mountains</ins>… <sup>21</sup>For then there will be <ins>great tribulation</ins>, <ins>such as has not been</ins>… <sup>22</sup>Unless <ins>those days were shortened</ins> [42 months], no flesh would be saved.” (Mt. 24:15-22)

C. A time of great trouble will come to Israel immediately after they enjoy a time of peace, safety, and prosperity (1 Thes. 5:2-3; cf. Isa. 2:7; Ezek. 38:8, 14, 16; Joel 2:3).

><sup>2</sup>…the <ins>day of the Lord</ins> so comes as a thief in the night. <sup>3</sup>For when they say, “<ins>Peace and safety</ins>!” <ins>then</ins> sudden <ins>destruction</ins> comes upon them…and they shall not escape. (1 Thes. 5:2-3)

><sup>8</sup>“In the latter years you will…[be] gathered on the mountains of Israel…<ins>all of them dwell safely</ins>… <sup>11</sup>and you [Antichrist] will…go up against a land of <ins>unwalled villages</ins>…to a <ins>peaceful</ins> people, who <ins>dwell safely</ins>… <sup>14</sup>On that day when My people Israel <ins>dwell safely</ins>…” (Ezek. 38:8-14)

D. I identify 7 situations for Jewish people living in Israel during the Tribulation (for 3½ years):

1. Some will ***remain in the land*** — protected by God (Isa. 4:3; Zech. 12:6-14; 14:2; Rev. 7:4).

2. Some will be ***anointed for ministry*** (Joel 2:28; Mic. 7:15; Rev. 11:3; 16:6; 18:24).

3. Some will be ***empowered for battle*** in military conflict (Is. 41:15 Zech. 9:13; 10:3-7; 12:6-8).

4. Some will ***flee into the wilderness*** of the nations (Ezek. 20:35; Mt. 24:16; Rev. 12:14).

5. Some will ***rebel against God*** (Ezek. 20:38; 34:16-22).

6. Some will be ***killed*** by the Antichrist’s forces (Dan. 7:21; 25; Zech. 11:16; 13:8; Rev. 13:7).

7. Some will be ***taken captive*** — Half of those in Jerusalem will be taken captive (Zech. 14:2).

><sup>2</sup>I will gather <ins>all the nations</ins>…against Jerusalem…<ins>half of the city shall go into captivity</ins>,… (Zech. 14:2)

## II. #1 SOME WILL REMAIN IN THE LAND—PROTECTED BY GOD

A. Some who remain in the land and those in the nations will be *supernaturally protected* (Isa. 4:3; 43:2; Zech. 14:2; Rev. 7:4; 12:14-16; cf. Ezek. 9:4-6; 28:25-26 were partially fulfilled).

><sup>2</sup>I will gather all the nations to battle against <ins>Jerusalem</ins>…half of the city shall go into captivity, but <ins>the remnant of the people shall not be cut off</ins> from the city. (Zech. 14:2)

><sup>3</sup>It shall come to pass that <ins>he who is left</ins> in Zion and <ins>remains in Jerusalem</ins> will be called holy — everyone who is recorded among <ins>the living</ins> in Jerusalem. (Isa. 4:3)

><sup>4</sup>And I heard the number of those who were sealed. One hundred and forty-four thousand of all the tribes of the children of Israel were sealed… (Rev. 7:4)

><sup>4</sup>…the LORD said to him [an angel], “Go through…the midst of Jerusalem [586 BC], and <ins>put a mark on</ins> the foreheads of the men <ins>who sigh and cry over</ins> all the abominations that are done within it.” <sup>5</sup>To the others [angels] He said…, “Go after him through the city and <ins>kill</ins> [via the army of Babylon]; do not let your eye spare, nor have any pity. <sup>6</sup>Utterly slay old and young men, maidens and little children…but <ins>do not come near anyone on whom is the mark</ins>…” (Ezek. 9:4-6)

><sup>14</sup>But the woman [remnant of Israel]…[went] into the wilderness…where <ins>she is nourished</ins> for a time and times and half a time [42 months], from the presence of the serpent… (Rev. 12:14)

><sup>2</sup>“I will be with you…when you walk through the fire, <ins>you shall not be burned</ins>…” (Isa. 43:2)

## III. #2 SOME WILL BE ANOINTED FOR MINISTRY

A. Some Jewish people will be *anointed for prophetic ministry* (Isa. 59:20-21; Joel 2:28; Mic. 7:15; Rev. 11:3; 16:6; 18:24). There will be a strong prophetic movement in Jerusalem.

><sup>3</sup>“And I will give power to my two witnesses [in Jerusalem], and <ins>they will prophesy</ins> one thousand two hundred and sixty days [1,260 days or 42 months], clothed in sackcloth.” (Rev. 11:3)

><sup>1</sup>“Blow the trumpet <ins>in Zion</ins> [Jerusalem]…the day of the LORD is coming…<sup>15</sup>Blow the trumpet in Zion, consecrate a fast…” <sup>18</sup><ins>Then</ins> the LORD will be zealous for <ins>His land</ins>… <sup>27</sup>“<ins>Then</ins> you shall know that I am in the midst of <ins>Israel</ins>… <sup>28</sup>I will pour out My Spirit on <ins>all flesh</ins>; your [Jerusalem] <ins>sons and daughters shall prophesy</ins>… <sup>32</sup>In <ins>Jerusalem</ins> there shall be <ins>deliverance</ins>… (Joel 2:1-32)

><sup>20</sup>“The Redeemer will come <ins>to Zion</ins> [Jerusalem]… <sup>21</sup>This is My covenant with them [Israel]: <ins>My Spirit who is upon you</ins>…from this time and <ins>forevermore</ins>.” (Isa. 59:20-21)

## IV. #3 SOME WILL BE EMPOWERED FOR BATTLE IN MILITARY CONFLICT

A. Some will be empowered for battle in military conflict especially at the end of the Tribulation (Isa. 11:14; 41:15; Obad. 18; Mic. 4:13; 5:8-9; Zech. 9:13, 15; 10:3-7; 12:6-8; 14:14).

><sup>4</sup>“<ins>In that day</ins>…I will open <ins>My eyes</ins> on…Judah, and will strike every horse…with <ins>blindness</ins>… <sup>6</sup>I will make the governors of Judah <ins>like a firepan</ins>…they shall <ins>devour all the surrounding peoples</ins>… <sup>8</sup>The one who is <ins>feeble</ins> among them…shall be <ins>like David</ins>…” (Zech. 12:4-8)

><sup>5</sup>They [Judah] shall be like <ins>mighty men</ins>, who <ins>tread down</ins> their enemies… (Zech. 10:5)

B. The Lord will make Israel into a mighty threshing sledge that will thresh mountains, speaking of destroying the Antichrist armies (Isa. 41:15). The “you” that God will make into a “threshing sledge” speaks of empowering the Israeli military to resist the Antichrist before Jesus returns. This occurs at the time of Israel’s national salvation—when they all rejoice in the Lord (Isa. 41:16).

><sup>12</sup>“<ins>Those who war against you</ins> shall be as nothing…<sup>14</sup>Fear not, you worm Jacob, you men of Israel! I will help you…<sup>15</sup>Behold, <ins>I will make you into a new threshing sledge</ins> with sharp teeth; you shall thresh the mountains and…make the hills like chaff. <sup>16</sup>You shall winnow them, the wind shall carry them away…<ins>you shall rejoice in the LORD</ins>, and glory in the Holy One of Israel.” (Isa. 41:12-16)
