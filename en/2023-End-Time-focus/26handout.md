
# Session #26 Seven Situations in Israel During the Tribulation (Pt. 2)

>“The friend of the Bridegroom…rejoices greatly because of the Bridegroom’s voice.” (Jn. 3:29)

## I. ISRAEL REFINED: 7 SITUATIONS FOR JEWS IN THE LAND IN THE 3½ YEARS

A. The Lord will bring 1/3 of Israel through the fire to refine them in bringing them to salvation. It is possible that over 5-6 million Jews worldwide will look to Jesus for salvation in the end times.

><sup>9</sup>I will bring the <ins>one-third through the fire</ins>…each will say, “The LORD is my God.” (Zech. 13:9)

><sup>26</sup>And so <ins>all Israel will be saved</ins>, as it is written… (Rom. 11:26)

B. The time of intense difficulty for Israel in the end times will not last longer than *42 months* (Dan. 12:1, 7; Mt. 24:21-22; cf. Dan. 7:25; 9:27; 12:7; Rev. 11:2-3; 12:6, 14; 13:5).

C. I identify 7 situations for Jewish people living in Israel during the Tribulation (for 3½ years):

1. Some will ***remain in the land*** — protected by God (Isa. 4:3; Zech. 12:6-14; 14:2; Rev. 7:4).

2. Some will be ***anointed for ministry*** (Joel 2:28; Mic. 7:15; Rev. 11:3; 16:6; 18:24).

3. Some will be ***empowered for battle*** in military conflict (Is. 41:15 Zech. 9:13; 10:3-7; 12:6-8).

4. Some will ***flee into the wilderness*** of the nations (Ezek. 20:35; Mt. 24:16; Rev. 12:14).

5. Some will ***rebel against God*** (Ezek. 20:38; 34:16-22).

6. Some will be ***killed*** by the Antichrist’s forces (Dan. 7:21; 25; Zech. 11:16; 13:8; Rev. 13:7).

7. Some will be ***taken captive*** — Half of those in Jerusalem will be taken captive (Zech. 14:2).

><sup>2</sup>I will gather <ins>all the nations</ins>…against Jerusalem…<ins>half of the city shall go into captivity</ins>,… (Zech. 14:2)

## II. #1 SOME WILL REMAIN IN THE LAND—PROTECTED BY GOD

A. Some who remain in the land and those in the nations will be *supernaturally protected* (Isa. 4:3; 43:2; Zech. 14:2; Rev. 7:4; 12:14-16; cf. Ezek. 9:4-6; 28:25-26 were partially fulfilled).

><sup>2</sup>I will gather all the nations to battle against <ins>Jerusalem</ins>…half of the city shall go into captivity, but <ins>the remnant of the people shall not be cut off</ins> from the city. (Zech. 14:2)

><sup>3</sup>It shall come to pass that <ins>he who is left</ins>…and <ins>remains in Jerusalem</ins> will be called holy. (Isa. 4:3)

## III. #2 SOME WILL BE ANOINTED FOR MINISTRY

A. Some Jewish people will be *anointed for prophetic ministry* (Isa. 59:20-21; Joel 2:28; Mic. 7:15; Rev. 11:3; 16:6; 18:24). There will be a strong prophetic movement in Jerusalem.

><sup>1</sup>“Blow the trumpet <ins>in Zion</ins> [Jerusalem]…the day of the LORD is coming…<sup>15</sup>Blow the trumpet in Zion, consecrate a fast…” <sup>18</sup><ins>Then</ins> the LORD will be zealous for <ins>His land</ins>… <sup>27</sup>“<ins>Then</ins> you shall know that I am in the midst of <ins>Israel</ins>… <sup>28</sup>I will pour out My Spirit on <ins>all flesh</ins>; your [Jerusalem] <ins>sons and daughters shall prophesy</ins>… <sup>32</sup>In <ins>Jerusalem</ins> there shall be <ins>deliverance</ins>… (Joel 2:1-32)

## IV. #3 SOME WILL BE EMPOWERED FOR BATTLE IN MILITARY CONFLICT

A. Some will be empowered for battle in military conflict especially at the end of the Tribulation (Isa. 11:14; 41:15; Obad. 18; Mic. 4:13; 5:8-9; Zech. 9:13, 15; 10:3-7; 12:6-8; 14:14).

B. The Lord will make Israel into a mighty threshing sledge that will thresh mountains, speaking of destroying the Antichrist armies (Isa. 41:15). The “you” that God will make into a “threshing sledge” speaks of empowering the Israeli military to resist the Antichrist before Jesus returns. This occurs at the time of Israel’s national salvation — when they all rejoice in the Lord (Isa. 41:16).

## V. #4 SOME WILL FLEE INTO THE WILDERNESS OF THE NATIONS

A. Some Jewish people will *flee as refugees* from Israel into the “wilderness of the nations” (Ezek. 20:35; Hos. 2:14; Mt. 24:16; Rev. 12:14).

><sup>15</sup>“…<ins>when</ins> you see the ‘<ins>abomination of desolation</ins>’…standing in the holy place [Jerusalem]… <sup>16</sup>“<ins>then</ins> let those <ins>who are in Judea flee to the mountains</ins>…[in the Middle East].” (Mt. 24:15-16)

B. God has ordained places of provision for Israel as she flees into the wilderness of the nations.

><sup>35</sup>I will bring you into the <ins>wilderness of the peoples</ins> [nations, NIV], and <ins>there I will plead My case with you face to face</ins>. <sup>36</sup>Just as I pleaded My case with your fathers in the wilderness of…Egypt, I will plead My case with you…<sup>37</sup>I will bring you into the bond of the covenant… (Ezek. 20:35-37)

C. The “wings of an eagle” spoke of Israel being brought to God at Mt. Sinai (Ex. 19:4).

><sup>13</sup>Now <ins>when</ins> the dragon [Satan]…<ins>persecuted the woman</ins> [the remnant of Israel]… <sup>14</sup>The woman was given <ins>two wings</ins> of a great eagle, that she might fly into <ins>the wilderness</ins> to <ins>her place</ins>, where she is <ins>nourished</ins> for [3½ years]… <sup>16</sup><ins>The earth helped the woman</ins>… (Rev. 12:13-16)

><sup>14</sup>“…I will <ins>allure</ins> her, <ins>will bring her into the wilderness</ins>, and speak comfort to her…<sup>15</sup> She shall sing there… <sup>16</sup>It shall be, in that day…that you will call Me ‘<ins>My Husband</ins>’…” (Hos. 2:14-16)

><sup>12</sup>…in that day the LORD will thresh [to gather Israel as grain]…<ins>you will be gathered one by one</ins>, O you <ins>children of Israel</ins> … <sup>13</sup>in that day, the <ins>great trumpet</ins> will be blown [at Jesus’ return]; they [Jewish refugees] will come, who are <ins>about to perish</ins> in the land of <ins>Assyria</ins>, and they who are <ins>outcasts</ins> [refugees] in <ins>Egypt</ins>, and shall worship the LORD [Jesus] in…<ins>Jerusalem</ins>. (Isa. 27:12-13)

## VI. #5 SOME WILL REBEL AGAINST GOD

A. Some Jewish people will *rebel against the Lord* (Ezek. 20:38; 34:16-22).

><sup>35</sup>“I will bring you into the wilderness of [the nations]…I will plead My case with you face to face… <sup>38</sup>I will <ins>purge the rebels from among you</ins>…they shall not enter the land of Israel.” (Ezek. 20:35-38)

><sup>16</sup>“…<ins>I will destroy</ins> the fat and the strong [wicked leaders], and <ins>feed them in judgment</ins>”… <sup>17</sup>“<ins>I shall judge</ins> between sheep and sheep, between rams and goats…<sup>20</sup>Behold, <ins>I Myself will judge</ins> between the fat and the lean sheep. <sup>21</sup>Because you have pushed with side and shoulder, butted all the weak ones with your horns, and scattered them abroad…” (Ezek. 34:16-21)

## VII. #6 SOME WILL BE KILLED BY THE ANTICHRIST’S FORCES

A. Some Jewish people *will killed* by the Antichrist’s forces (Zech. 13:8; cf. Isa. 3:24-26; Dan. 7:21, 25; Zech. 11:16). The Hebrew word *ha'arets* or *eretz* can mean “the earth” or “the land.” The context determines which is best. The phrase *“haa’ erets”* (all the land) occurs 3 times in Zechariah (4:10; 5:6; 13:8), and translated as *earth* in 4:10 and 5:6. The timing of the 2/3 dying is the same as when the 1/3 are saved. Up to 50% of the Gentiles will die in the Tribulation (Rev. 6:8; 9:15).

><sup>8</sup>“And it shall come to pass in <ins>all the land</ins> [earth],” Says the LORD, “That <ins>two-thirds in it shall be cut off and die</ins>, but one-third shall be left in it: <sup>9</sup>I will bring the <ins>one-third through the fire</ins>…they will call on My name…and each one will say, ‘The LORD is my God.’” (Zech. 13:8-9)

B. Some will be killed by the Antichrist’s forces (Dan. 7:21, 25; Zech. 11:16; 13:8).

><sup>21</sup>“I was watching; and the same horn [the Antichrist] was <ins>making war</ins> against the saints, and <ins>prevailing against them</ins>, <sup>22</sup>until the Ancient of Days [the Father] came, and a judgment was made in favor of the saints…<sup>25</sup>He [the Antichrist] shall …<ins>persecute the saints</ins>…Then the saints shall be <ins>given into his hand</ins> [to oppress and kill them] for a time and times and half a time [3½ years]. (Dan. 7:21-25)

## VIII. #7 SOME WILL BE TAKEN CAPTIVE

A. Some Jewish people will be *taken captive* — Zechariah prophesied that half of the Jewish people in Jerusalem will be taken captive (Zech. 14:2). Current, nearly 600,000 Jews live in Jerusalem.

><sup>2</sup>I will gather <ins>all the nations</ins> to battle against Jerusalem; <ins>the city shall be taken</ins>…<ins>half of the city shall go into captivity</ins>, but the remnant of the people shall not be cut off from the city. <sup>3</sup><ins>Then</ins> the LORD will go forth and fight against those nations… (Zech. 14:2-3)

1. The Old Testament addresses Jewish people being scattered, persecuted, and/or taken captive at various times in history, yet particularly in context to the generation of Jesus’ return (Ps. 102:20; Isa. 11:11-14, 16; 14:1-3; 19:23; 27:12-13; 40:11; 42:7, 16, 22; 45:14; 49:9-12, 21, 24-26; 60:10, 12, 14; 61:1, 5; Jer. 30:3, 8, 10, 17; 31:16, 23; Ezek. 34:27; 39:23-29; Hos. 11:11; Joel 3:1-2, 8; Amos 9:14; Mic. 2:12-13; 4:6-7; 5:6; 7:12; Zeph. 3:19-20; Zech. 9:11-12; 10:10-11; 13:8; 14:2; cf. Mt. 25:43; Lk. 21:24).

2. Moses’ prophecy of Israel being scattered and in captivity (Deut. 28:64) was partially fulfilled at the hands of Assyria (721 BC), Babylon (586 BC), and Rome (AD 70).

3. Christians enduring persecution in end-time prophecy must also be understood (Mt. 24:9; Lk. 21:16-19; Jn. 15:18-25; Rev. 6:9-11; 12:11, 17; 13:7, 10; 16:5-7; 17:6; 18:24; 19:2; 20:4).

B. One of the most offensive aspects of the Lord’s end-time plan — to a humanistic mind set — is that He will allow some Jewish people and Christians go into captivity — some in prison camps.

><sup>6</sup>“…I will give You [Messiah] as a covenant to the people…<sup>7</sup><ins>to bring out prisoners from the prison</ins>, those who sit in darkness from the <ins>prison house</ins>…” <sup>22</sup>But this is a people robbed and plundered; <ins>all of them</ins> are <ins>snared in holes</ins>, and they are <ins>hidden in prison houses</ins>… <sup>23</sup><ins>Who</ins> among you will give ear to this?… <sup>24</sup>Who gave Jacob for plunder, and Israel to the robbers? <ins>Was it not the LORD</ins>, He against whom we have sinned? (Isa. 42:6-24)
